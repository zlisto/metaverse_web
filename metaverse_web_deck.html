The idea

Walk out of a Yale classroom and into a Cambridge kebab house.

Each world is its own live website, built by anyone, on any server. A door in one world points to another world’s address. Walking through it is clicking a link.

Protocol v0.1

Four messages make a world linkable

1 · manifest

Each world serves a small JSON file: its name, spawn points and portals.

2 · enter

Browser → world: here is who I am and which spawn point to use.

3 · navigate

World → browser: I walked into a portal to this address.

4 · presence

World → browser: where I am now, such as “near the counter”.

The manifest

A world describes itself

Served at a well-known path, like robots.txt. A 3D browser reads it, and so can an AI agent.

// /.well-known/metaverse.json
{
  "name": "Darul Kebab",
  "protocol": "mvw/0.1",
  "spawns": { "front_door": "…" },
  "portals": [{
    "id": "to_yale", "label": "Yale SOM",
    "href": "http://localhost:5173",
    "spawn": "courtyard"
  }]
}

From Evans Hall to dinner in five steps

2

3

The standard, for builders

Six steps put any world on the Metaverse Web

1 Build it

Any 3D website, any engine: three.js, Babylon, Unity WebGL.

2 Spawn points

Read ?spawn= on load. Always have a default.

3 Add mvw.js

One small file: says ready, reports where you are, hands portal trips to the browser.

4 Manifest

Serve .well-known/metaverse.json: name, description, spawns, portals.

5 Host it

Anywhere static: GitHub Pages, Netlify, your own server.

6 Link both ways

Portal to a friend’s address and spawn; they add one back.

The full spec is PROTOCOL.md; world_starter/ is a template that already does all six.

Linking to Yale SOM

Your world, then one portal to mine

Put a portal in your world whose address is Yale SOM and whose spawn is one of its arrival points. List it in your manifest too.

Then send me your address and a spawn id. I add a portal back, and the two worlds are joined.

// your manifest’s portals
{
  "id": "to_yale",
  "label": "Yale SOM",
  "href": "https://zlisto.github.io/
    yale_som_virtual/",
  "spawn": "default"
}

// in your three.js world
<Portal x={0} z={-4}
  href="https://zlisto.github.io/yale_som_virtual/"
  spawn="default" back="lobby"
  label="Yale SOM" />

The idea is old. What changed in 2026 is who walks the web.

What is new

A web built for people and AI agents to walk together.

Every world explains itself in plain JSON, so an agent can enter, look around, talk to the owner and follow a portal, just as you do.
